# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for contributors of
every background and level of experience. Discussion should focus on evidence,
reproducibility, user impact, and the work itself.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, patient, and specific in reviews and issue discussions.
- Respect differing experiences and technical viewpoints.
- Share enough evidence for others to reproduce technical claims.
- Protect API credentials, customer data, private catalogs, and conversation traces.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope without personal attacks.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disclosure of private
information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact a maintainer through the repository.
For sensitive reports, use GitHub's private security-advisory form. Maintainers
may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or ban accounts when needed
to protect the community. Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as
practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in
public community spaces.
